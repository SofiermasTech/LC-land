Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy explains how LMND Card (“we”, “us”, “our”) uses cookies and similar technologies on the LMND Card website, landing pages and in the LMND Card application (together, the “Services”).

By accessing or using the Services, or by filling out web forms and registering as a user, you provide your informed and conscious consent to the use of cookies and similar technologies under this Cookie Policy, unless you disable them via your browser or our consent tools.

1. What cookies and similar technologies are

Cookies are small text files that are placed on your device (computer, mobile phone or tablet) when you visit websites. They are widely used to make websites work or function more efficiently, as well as to provide reporting information and support security and personalization.

LMND Card may also use similar technologies such as local storage, pixels, tags, SDKs and device identifiers, which work in a way comparable to cookies.

Cookies set by LMND Card are “first‑party cookies”, while cookies set by third parties (for example, analytics or advertising partners) are “third‑party cookies”.​

2. What data we collect via cookies

In addition to the personal data described in our main Privacy Policy, LMND Card may automatically collect the following information with the help of cookies and similar technologies:​

  • IP address and approximate location
  • Device identifiers (for example, MAC address or mobile device ID)
  • Browser type, version and language
  • Information from cookies and local storage
  • Information about how you use the Services (pages viewed, clicks, access time, referrer URL, etc.)
  • Anonymized data collected via Internet statistics services (for example, analytics tools)​

This data may be combined with other information you provide to us in order to improve the Services, prevent fraud and comply with applicable law.

3. Types of cookies we use and purposes

We may use the following categories of cookies on LMND Card:

3.1. Strictly necessary cookies

These cookies are essential for the operation of the Services and for the provision of core functionality, such as keeping you logged in, processing your requests, ensuring security and preventing fraud. Without these cookies the Services may not function properly.

3.2. Performance and analytics cookies

These cookies help us understand how users interact with the Services, which pages are visited, and how the Services perform. We use this information (often in aggregated form) to maintain and improve the quality, stability and content of LMND Card.

3.3. Functional cookies

These cookies allow the Services to remember your preferences and settings (for example, language, region, saved details where permitted). If you disable these cookies, some personalized features may not work as intended.

3.4. Advertising and marketing cookies

These cookies may be used by us or our partners to show you more relevant advertising, limit how often you see a particular ad and measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns. They may track your browser or device across different websites and applications to build a profile of your interests.

4. Cookies and similar technologies in the LMND Card application

When you use the LMND Card application, we may use SDKs, device identifiers, local storage and similar technologies for the purposes described above: to ensure security, perform analytics, guard against fraud, and provide you with services and support. Control of such technologies may be available in your device settings, within the application itself, and via the consent mechanisms we provide where required by applicable law.

5. Legal basis for the use of cookies

The legal basis for our use of cookies and similar technologies depends on the type of cookies and the applicable law of the country where the Services are provided.

  • Strictly necessary cookies are used based on our legitimate interest in operating secure and efficient Services and fulfilling our obligations to users.​
  • Non‑essential cookies (such as analytics or advertising cookies) are used with your consent where required by applicable law.

You can withdraw your consent at any time by changing your cookie settings, as described in Section 6 of this Cookie Policy.

6. How you can control cookies

You have several options to manage or limit the use of cookies and similar technologies:

6.1. Browser settings

Most web browsers allow you to block or delete cookies, to accept certain types of cookies, or to be notified when a cookie is set. The relevant settings are usually found in the “Options”, “Settings” or “Privacy” section of your browser.

6.2. Cookie banner / preference center

When you first visit LMND Card, you may see a cookie banner that allows you to accept or reject certain categories of cookies. Where available, you can change your preferences later via the “Cookie settings” link in the footer or within the app.

6.3. Third‑party opt‑outs

Some analytics and advertising providers offer their own opt‑out tools. Information about such tools may be available on their websites or in our detailed cookie table.

If you disable some or all cookies, the functionality of the Services may be limited and certain features may not be available.

7. Data storage period

Cookies and similar technologies are stored for as long as necessary to achieve the purposes for which they are used, but no longer than required by applicable law or our internal policies. After the expiration of the storage period, cookies are deleted or anonymized so that it is no longer possible to identify the personal data subject.

8. Changes to this Cookie Policy

We may update this Cookie Policy from time to time, for example, to reflect changes in the cookies and technologies we use or to comply with new legal requirements. The date of the latest version will be indicated at the top of this document, and the updated version will be published on the LMND Card website.

If you continue to use the Services after changes to this Cookie Policy, you agree to the updated version, unless you withdraw your consent where applicable.
